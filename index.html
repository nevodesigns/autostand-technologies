01

Two engineering problems, one company

Most accessory businesses buy finished goods and resell them. AutoStand does not. We designed the stand, then designed the line that produces it, because in this market the cost of manufacturing is the business.

The product

A durable, ergonomic, foldable stand with an adjustable viewing angle, universal device compatibility and optional custom branding for businesses ordering at volume.

The machine

A semi-automatic production system that takes raw sheet and moulded stock through six stages to a finished, inspected unit with minimal human intervention.

02

The stand

Designed for the way phones are actually used: propped on a desk through a lecture, a call, a shift behind a counter.

Adjustable viewing angle
Position the device at the angle the task needs, from near-upright for video calls to shallow for typing and reading.
Foldable and portable
Collapses flat. Fits a bag, a backpack or a pocket without adding meaningful bulk.
Universal compatibility
Supports most smartphones and small tablets, with or without a case fitted.
Non-slip support
Anti-slip contact surfaces at the cradle and the base keep the device secure under touch input.
Custom branding
Logo and promotional message customisation, which is what turns a consumer accessory into a corporate order.

03

The production line

Six stages, one pass. Material enters at stage one and leaves stage six as a finished, dimensionally verified unit.

  1. Material feeding

    Raw plastic sheet or moulded components are introduced into the machine.

  2. Detection and alignment

    Sensors detect material position and ensure precise alignment before processing begins.

  3. Processing

    Cutting, shaping, drilling and finishing operations are performed automatically.

  4. Assembly

    Individual components are brought together automatically or semi-automatically.

  5. Quality inspection

    Sensors verify dimensional accuracy and product consistency at each unit.

  6. Collection

    Completed stands are deposited into a collection tray ready for packaging.

06

What comes next

The current machine is semi-automatic by design: prove the process, then close the loop around it.

  1. AI quality inspection

    Vision-based defect detection replacing sensor threshold checks, targeting zero-defect output.

  2. Remote monitoring

    IoT telemetry and machine diagnostics, so a line can be supervised without standing beside it.

  3. Mobile control interface

    Start, stop and reconfigure a run from a phone.

  4. Production analytics

    Throughput, yield and downtime surfaced in real time rather than reconstructed afterwards.

  5. Automated packaging

    Closing the last manual step between collection tray and shipping carton.

  6. Multi-product capability

    Retooling the same line for adjacent accessories, which is where the machine becomes the product.
